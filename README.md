# AgriLink – Connecting Farmers to Market Opportunities

**Project:** AgriLink  
**Category:** Digital Agriculture / Food Supply Chain  
**Developer:** Nebil Kebede  
**Project Status:** Working Mobile Application Prototype  
**Initial Focus:** Tomato Trading in Hawassa and Shashemene, Ethiopia

---

## 1. Project Overview

AgriLink is a mobile-based digital agriculture platform designed to connect farmers, agricultural buyers, and transport providers through a single application.

The project aims to improve farmers' access to market opportunities, simplify agricultural product discovery, and support the coordination of product orders and deliveries.

Initially focusing on tomato trading in Hawassa and Shashemene, Ethiopia, AgriLink provides digital tools that help farmers list their available products, enable buyers to discover and order agricultural produce, and allow transport providers to manage delivery opportunities.

AgriLink aims to make agricultural trading more accessible, connected, and efficient.

## 2. Problem Statement

Farmers and agricultural market participants can experience several challenges:

- Difficulty finding suitable buyers for available agricultural products.
- Limited access to market opportunities and product information.
- Challenges coordinating product orders and transportation.
- Fragmented communication between farmers, buyers, and transport providers.

AgriLink addresses these challenges by bringing key agricultural supply-chain participants together through a digital mobile platform.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Improve farmers' access to potential buyers.
- Make agricultural products easier to discover.
- Support digital product listing and inventory management.
- Simplify order and delivery coordination.
- Connect farmers, buyers, and transport providers through one platform.

## 4. Main Features

### Farmer Module
- Farmer account and profile management.
- Create and manage agricultural product listings.
- Specify product prices and available quantities.
- View and manage orders.

### Buyer Module
- Browse available agricultural products.
- View product details and prices.
- Place product orders.
- Manage order information.

### Transporter Module
- View available delivery jobs.
- Accept delivery assignments.
- Manage assigned deliveries.
- Update delivery status.

### Agent Module
- Support farmer onboarding.
- Assist with farmer verification.
- Support delivery confirmation and field activities.

*Note: The availability of individual features depends on the current implementation of the application.*

### Shared Platform Features
- Phone number-based OTP authentication.
- Role-based user interfaces.
- Product and inventory management.
- Order management.
- Delivery coordination.
